Connect your fediverse server to Mwmbl Social

Mwmbl Social is a search engine for the fediverse that indexes only public posts and accounts whose authors opted in. Mwmbl, the non-profit open source search engine, runs an instance of it at social.mwmbl.org that any server can connect to as a FASP (Fediverse Auxiliary Service Provider). You can also run your own.

What connecting gives your server

  • Account search and follow recommendations from across the fediverse. Today these are the only things Mastodon asks a FASP for, and only from version 4.5 with EXPERIMENTAL_FEATURES=fasp set. The FASP specs have no post search yet.
  • Data sharing: your server tells us about its opted-in public posts and accounts, so your users can be found beyond your server, including in Mwmbl search as that comes online.

Consent

Only fully public posts are indexed: never unlisted, quiet public, followers-only or direct ones. An account must have discoverable turned on, and its posts also need indexable (in Mastodon: Preferences > Public profile > Privacy and reach). Turning these off, or deleting a post, removes it. See the privacy policy for the details.
