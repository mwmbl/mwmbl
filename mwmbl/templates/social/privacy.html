Privacy policy for social.mwmbl.org

social.mwmbl.org is run by Mwmbl, the non-profit open source search engine. It runs Mwmbl Social, open source software (source, AGPL-3.0) that indexes fediverse posts and accounts whose authors opted in. This page covers social.mwmbl.org. Your Mwmbl account is covered by Mwmbl's data privacy policy.

What is indexed

  • Only fully public posts. Unlisted, quiet public, followers-only and direct posts never are.
  • An account is indexed only if it has discoverable turned on, and its posts only if it also has indexable turned on. In Mastodon these are under Preferences > Public profile > Privacy and reach.
  • Lemmy accounts don't have these settings, so a Lemmy author who hasn't set them counts as agreeing. Where one is explicitly off, that is respected.
  • Following @mwmbl-social@social.mwmbl.org opts you in for any of these settings your account doesn't have. A setting that is explicitly off still wins, and unfollowing withdraws the opt-in.

Where it comes from

  • Servers whose admins connected them and turned on data sharing tell us about posts and accounts.
  • Lemmy communities, followed only when their instance's admins agreed, and accounts that follow us.
  • Lemmy post and comment addresses found by Mwmbl's crawler. These are fetched only where the instance's robots.txt allows it.

Whatever the source, we fetch each post or account from its own server and check the rules above before indexing it.

What we store

  • For a post: its address, its author's address, its title and language if it has them, when it was published and last checked, and an extract of up to about 300 characters of its text, including any content warning and hashtags.
  • For an account: its address and handle, display name, a shortened bio, and the languages it posts in.
  • What is needed to run the service: the servers connected to it and their settings, a queue of addresses to fetch, what we know about each host (its software and robots.txt), removals still being applied, the accounts that follow us and when, the accounts and communities we follow, and removal requests.

We never store non-public posts anywhere, not even in logs. Mwmbl Social does not keep the searches connected servers send it.

Who sees it

Indexed posts and accounts are public already. They are shown to connected servers in answer to account searches and follow recommendations, through Mwmbl Social's search API, and, once that is built, in Mwmbl search. Mwmbl will not copy them into its own web index, so removals here apply everywhere.

How things are removed

  • Deleting a post, making it non-public, or turning off discoverable or indexable removes it. When a connected server tells us, that happens straight away. Everything indexed is also checked again at least once a week, which catches the rest.
  • Unfollowing @mwmbl-social@social.mwmbl.org removes posts indexed because you followed it.
  • To have a post, an account or a whole domain removed for good, email info@mwmbl.org with its address. It will not be indexed again.
  • A server admin can disconnect their server on the connect page, and should also remove Mwmbl Social from their server's admin panel.

If you connect a server

Mwmbl records, with your Mwmbl account, the server address you entered, its registration with Mwmbl Social, the approval link and fingerprint, and when you connected and disconnected it. Other Mwmbl users can't see these; Mwmbl's operators can, as they can the rest of your account. Mwmbl Social itself does not know which Mwmbl account registered a server.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@mwmbl.org.
