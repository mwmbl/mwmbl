The odd bad item

A crawler that slips in the occasional spam page keeps a normal median. What changes is how often its comparisons land in the network's own lowest-agreeing tail, counting only copies crawled within a day of each other. Every crawler should land there about as often as the typical one, give or take chance, and chance narrows as volume grows: the funnel is that allowance. A dot above the funnel lands in the tail more often than chance explains.
